### (1) Bible Verse Wallpapers/Prints: Digital vs. Physical

**Digital wallpapers on Etsy/Gumroad:**

- Most sell for $2–$8 per file [printshrimp]
- Even at 100 sales/month, that's only $200–$600 before fees [printshrimp]
- Digital products face a "race to the bottom" as sellers bundle thousands of files for $3–5 [printshrimp]
- Etsy fees: $0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction + 3% + $0.25 processing = ~11% total [listybox]

**Physical prints via Print-on-Demand:**

- 16×20" prints sell for $15–$25 with $15–$25 profit per sale after fees [printshrimp]
- 24×36" premium: $30–$40 [printshrimp]
- Physical has higher perceived value, less refund risk, and better margins [printshrimp]

**Realistic earnings for small creators (<10k followers):**

- Digital-only: $50–$300/month at best without external traffic [printshrimp]
- Physical POD: $300–$1,500/month if you list 20–50 designs and get Pinterest/Instagram traffic [printshrimp]

### (2) Christian Sleep Music Packs

**Direct digital pack sales are rare. The market works differently:**

- **DistroKid (streaming):** To earn $1,000/month, you need ~250,000 streams at $0.004/stream [reddit]
- Small Christian music artists typically earn **$20–$200/month** from streaming unless they hit viral growth [reddit]
- **Pray.com** (major Christian app) pays via premium subscriptions (~$30/month for 6M daily users) but doesn't sell individual packs [npr]
- **YouTube** sleep music channels earn via AdSense, not direct pack sales [youtube]
- **Verified case:** One small creator crossed $25,000 in streams + downloads across DistroKid/TuneCore/CD Baby over time, not monthly [instagram]

**Bottom line:** Sleep music is **not viable as standalone digital packs**; it requires streaming scale or YouTube ad revenue.
